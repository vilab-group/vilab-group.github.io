We read images the eye cannot: through the body, and from the sky.

MAVI Lab builds machine learning for medical scans and aerial imagery — two domains that share the same hard problems: dense pixels, scarce labels, and decisions that have to hold up outside the lab.

Research

Four lines of work

Details
MED

Medical image segmentation and diagnosis

Volumetric segmentation, lesion detection and report grounding across CT, MRI and radiography — under the label scarcity and annotation noise that real clinical archives actually have.

3D segmentationWeak supervisionMulti-modal registrationDICOM
AER

Aerial and satellite perception

Small-object detection, change detection and land-cover mapping on imagery that shifts with sensor, altitude, season and atmosphere.

Change detectionMultispectralDomain shiftGeo-referencing
REP

Representation learning across imaging domains

Self-supervised pre-training and foundation-model adaptation that transfer between the two domains — both are dense, high-resolution and starved of labels, and they turn out to teach each other.

Self-supervisedFoundation modelsTransfer learning
TRU

Trustworthy and efficient deployment

Calibration, out-of-distribution detection and compression, so a model behaves predictably on a hospital workstation or an onboard drone processor.

UncertaintyOOD detectionEdge inferenceModel compression
